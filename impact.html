How we count

We want our numbers to mean something. Here's how each stat above is calculated.

  • People reached — cumulative count of individuals we've spoken with directly at community awareness events, plus documented digital touchpoints.
  • Community awareness events — every in-person outreach event where our team distributed multilingual Medi-Cal materials or answered renewal questions.
  • Found this site helpful — percentage of respondents who rated the site "somewhat" or "very" helpful in the feedback survey below. Updates live as new responses come in.
  • Languages supported — the languages this site is available in. Native-speaker review is ongoing.
  • AI assistant conversations — distinct chat sessions where AccessBot was opened and at least one question was asked.
  • California counties covered — verified contact information available for all 58 counties via the official DHCS County Contact List.
