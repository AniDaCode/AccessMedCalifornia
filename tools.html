What goes wrong

The most common reasons people lose coverage.

Most coverage losses aren't because someone is no longer eligible — they're procedural. Here's what to watch for.

Most common

Missed mail

You moved (or your mailbox is unreliable) and never saw the renewal packet.

Prevention: Update your address at BenefitsCal.com whenever you move.
Very common

Missed deadline

You got the packet but waited — and the deadline came and went.

Prevention: Submit the day you receive your packet. Online takes about 30 minutes.
Common

Missing documents

You submitted on time, but the county needed one more thing and the follow-up window closed.

Prevention: Check your BenefitsCal account weekly during your renewal month.
Myth vs Fact

Bust the myths that cost people coverage.

Misinformation is one of the top reasons eligible people don't renew. Here are the ones we hear most.

Myth
"Medi-Cal renews automatically — I don't need to do anything."
Fact

Pandemic-era auto-renewals ended. You must actively complete your renewal every 12 months.

Myth
"If I missed my deadline, I have to apply all over again."
Fact

DHCS materials describe a possible 90-day window to submit a late renewal and have coverage reinstated if you still qualify.

Myth
"I have to pay a fee to renew or get help."
Fact

Renewal is 100% free. Anyone asking for payment is running a scam — report to (800) 822-6222.

Myth
"I make too much money to qualify."
Fact

Income limits are often higher than people think. California uses several different eligibility programs — apply and let the county decide.

Myth
"If I lose Medi-Cal, I have no other options."
Fact

You may qualify for Covered California, where many people get $0 or low-cost plans. Applications often screen for both automatically.

Myth
"Using Medi-Cal will affect my immigration case."
Fact

Under current federal guidance, most Medi-Cal use does not count against public charge. For specific cases, consult a qualified legal resource.
